# what is third space

Third Space is a Hack Club program where small teams build real software together over 14 weeks

## what third space actually is

Third Space is a 14-week program where you and 1-3 friends each log 10+ hours a week building projects together. 
Keeping the streak for all 8 weeks earn each team member a $1000 grant for ont piece of tech, and what you pick is up to you.

Teams don't need matching skills, one person can handle the backend, another does the art, someone else does the frontend. and no experience is required. The point is just shipping.

## how it works

A Third Space team is meade up of two to four people. Each person on the team needs to log 10 or more hours a week working on the project.
The catch, and the part that keeps everyone honest, is the streak: everyone has to keep logging hours consistently for all 8 weeks.
Hit that streak and every single member of the team gets a $1000 grant to spend on one piece of tech of their choosing.

## the schedule for thirdspace
- **21 Aug to 13 Sept** :- Prep weeks where you can work towards shop items
- **14 Sept to 8 Nov** :- Actual weeks to work towards your tech grant
- **9 Nov to 29 Nov** :- Bonus weeks where you can get more stamps for the same effort put in!

## get started

To join, sign up at [thirdspace.hackclub.com](https://thirdspace.hackclub.com) using Hack Club Auth and invite your team. Once your team is in, the
clock started on the 14 weeks program and the 8 weeks streak that unlocks the grant.
