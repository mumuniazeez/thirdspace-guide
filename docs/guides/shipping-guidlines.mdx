# shipping guidelines

all you need to know before you ship.

a more detailed shipping guide will be update soon, but this should be enough for now.

## tracking your hours

All software and hardware time must be tracked on Hackatime (Software) or Lapse (Hardware).
Only actual coding or art and design work counts, in line with most YSWS programs,
so time spent planning, researching, and non-building activities doesn't count towards your hours.

## team contribution split

To ensure fairness within the team and to make sure every contribute, every team member must contribute at least 20% of the total project's hours.

A split like 10%, 40%, 40%, 10% across a four person team isn't allowed, but something closer to 20%, 30%, 25%, 25% is fine.

The rule exists to keep the work genuinely shared rather then carried by one or two people.

## working across projects

Team members can work on more than one project, but the 20% minimum total project time rule still applies per person.

Third Space is built around teamwork, so splitting of to work solo on something separate isn't allowed.

## AI Usage

AI shouldn't be relied on to build your project. About 30% AI usage is allowed, meaning the bulk os the work should be your own.
