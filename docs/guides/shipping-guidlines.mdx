# shipping guidelines

all you need to know before you ship.

a more detailed shipping guide will be update soon, but this should be enough for now.

## tracking your hours

All software and hardware time must be tracked on Hackatime (Software) or Lapse (Hardware).
Only actual coding or art and design work counts, in line with most YSWS programs,
so time spent planning, researching, and non-building activities doesn't count towards your hours.

## team contribution split

To ensure fairness within the team and to make sure every contribute, every team member must contribute at least 20% of the total project's hours.

A split like 10%, 40%, 40%, 10% across a four person team isn't allowed, but something closer to 20%, 30%, 25%, 25% is fine.

The rule exists to keep the work genuinely shared rather then carried by one or two people.

## working across projects

Team members can work on more than one project, but the 20% minimum total project time rule still applies per person.

Third Space is built around teamwork, so splitting of to work solo on something separate isn't allowed.

## AI usage

AI shouldn't be relied on to build your project. About 30% AI usage is allowed, meaning the bulk of the work should be your own.

## art time

Art can make up to 50% of your personal time, not the team's total time. This applies individually, not across the whole team's hours.

## devlogs (hardware projects)

Devlogs are only required for hardware projects, and they need to be posted every 3 to 5 hours of work logged.

## deadlines

Weekly deadlines lands at 11:59 PM ET every Sunday. There are no extensions or exceptions unless stated by the organizers.

## TL;DR

- Track all software hours on Hackatime or Lapse. Only actual coding or art/design counts.
- Every team member must contribute at least 20% of total project hours.
- You can work on multiple projects, but the 20% minimum still applies per project.
- AI use is capped around 30%. Don't rely on it to build the project.
- Art can be up to 50% of your own personal time (not the team's).
- Hardware projects need a devlog every 3-5 hours.
- Weekly deadline: 11:59 PM ET, every Sunday. No extensions.
